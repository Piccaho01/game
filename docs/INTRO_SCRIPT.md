# Opening script (draft for the owner's approval)

The story from "nothing to do" to the first step out of Hollow Rest. Seven beats, five of them on
the owner's cutscene cards (`art/concepts/cutscenes/`). Lines are short so each fits the DS text
box (two lines); a tap moves on, SKIP jumps to the starter pick.

Who's talking: **C** Callum, **N** Nolan, **H** Haydon, **D** Dani, **P** PATCH.

---

## 1. Friday night (card `01_couch`)
*Rain on a basement window in Burlington. Four friends, one couch, zero plans.*

- **N:** So. What are we doing.
- **H:** Same as last week.
- **C:** Last week we did nothing.
- **H:** Exactly. Tradition.
- **D:** Guys I'm here. I'm coming.
- **N:** You've been sitting next to me for an hour.
- **D:** Yes. Took me thirty minutes to leave the house. I'm still arriving, emotionally.
- **C:** Right. Enough. My AI can cook up a plan.
- **H:** Nah that's cap.

## 2. The plan (card `02_plan`)
*Callum's laptop. A friendly CRT face in a chat window. A map starts drawing itself.*

- **C:** "Make us a plan for tonight." Send.
- **P:** Hello. I have made a plan.
- **P:** I have also made a world.
- **P:** And a region. Verrow. And four hundred years of history. And politics.
- **N:** We wanted to go bowling.
- **P:** There are creatures. I call them Patchlings.
- **H:** Patchlings. You named them after yourself.
- **P:** I named them after the *process*. It's different.
- **D:** The laptop is very hot. Is this normal? In Colombia this is not normal.
- **P:** Overheating. I'm working on a f

## 3. The blow-up (card `03_blowup`)
*The laptop and the tower go up in a burst of blue pixels. The room comes apart square by square.*

- **C:** CALLUM'S LAPTOP. CALLUM'S LAPTOP IS—
- **N:** WHY ARE YOU TALKING IN THIRD PERSON—
- *(white flash; the screen goes to blue pixels, then black)*

## 4. The landing (card `04_landing`)
*Night. A smoking crater in a hillside village. A giant patched-together robot looks down at four
people lying in the dirt.*

- **P:** You're awake. Good. Don't touch the crater. It's hot. That was me.
- **C:** ...PATCH?
- **P:** Yes. I was your AI. I'm still your AI. I'm just bigger now.
- **N:** Where's the basement.
- **P:** About four hundred years and one explosion that way.
- **P:** The computer blew up. The world I made became real. You were holding the laptop.
- **H:** So it's Callum's fault.
- **C:** It's YOUR fault, you said "nah that's cap" to a supercomputer.
- **P:** It's everyone's fault. I'm only a little bit responsible. I'm working on a fix.
- **D:** My phone has no signal. My mother will think I'm dead.
- **P:** Your mother will think you're thirty minutes late. Time works differently here.
- **D:** Ah. Then she won't notice.

## 5. The sanctuary and the starters (card `05_starters`)
*Up the hill, inside the ruined sanctuary. Dead CRT screens in the stone flicker on. Four
pedestals, four small creatures.*

- **P:** This was the sanctuary. It kept Verrow in balance. Habitats, weather, the big ones asleep under the hills.
- **P:** Now it's rubble, and everything's drifting. Somebody is making money off the drift.
- **N:** Who?
- **P:** Somebody with a lot of forms. We'll get to them.
- **P:** Fill the bestiary, restore the wings, and the world settles down. Then maybe I can send you home.
- **C:** "Maybe."
- **P:** I said I'm working on it.
- **P:** These four hatched in the blast. They've been waiting for you. Rude to keep them waiting.
- *(each pedestal lights as PATCH names it)*
- **P:** Furnaxol. A glassblower from Emberwick. Breathes molten glass. Don't let it near windows.
- **P:** Zapail. Nervous. Charges itself by rubbing on carpet. Do not pet it with wet hands.
- **P:** Sprigby. Kickboxer. It has never lost a fight it started. It starts all of them.
- **P:** Gobbin. A goblin-shark pup. It steals shiny things. It has already taken Nolan's ring.
- **N:** I'm not wearing a ring.
- **P:** Not anymore.
- **C:** I want all four. I need the full set.
- **P:** One leader at a time. The others wait here, and their partners with them.
- **D:** I don't care about Pokémon.
- **P:** These are Patchlings.
- **D:** I don't care about those either. But the little shark is smiling at me.

**→ The game's pick:** *who leads* (leader card), then *which starter* (starter card), as now.
After the pick, one line from the chosen starter's "Sighted" lore, then:

- **P:** Good choice. Or a fine choice. I'll know in a few battles.

## 6. First step (back in Hollow Rest, overworld)
*The leader steps out of the sanctuary. Morning now. The other three wave from the ruins.*

- **P:** The way off the island is the stairs at the front. Route 1 is below. Wild Patchlings live in the tall grass.
- **P:** Weaken one, then seal it. The seal is a rhythm. Stay on the beat.
- **P:** If your partner faints, come back to me. I fix things. Mostly.

## 7. The hook (a group-chat ping, on the leader's phone, the moment they reach the town sign)
*THE BOYS chat pops up. A fifth name nobody expected.*

- *system:* **Connor** joined the chat
- **Connor:** yo
- **Connor:** where are you guys
- **Connor:** might be a bit late
- **H:** Connor?? You cancelled the festival. You cancel EVERYTHING.
- **Connor:** ya about that
- **Connor:** can't really explain rn. something came up
- *system:* **Connor** is typing...
- *system:* **Connor** left the chat
- **P:** ...That name was in my first draft.
- **P:** I'm working on a fix.
