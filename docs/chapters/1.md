## Story beat: the Tally rain-checked the rain
- On Route 1's west hilltop stands the **ruined storm-rod**: the keepers' lightning rod that
  called the storms the hills need. A dim brass lantern hangs off it, an eye inside, barely lit.
- Next to it the Tally has bolted on the **Cloud Clamp**: a grey box with a stamping arm that
  stamps every cloud that drifts over RAIN CHECK. They spliced the rod's cable into it. It hasn't
  rained on Route 1 in years ("Precipitation: rescheduled").
- **Tally moment:** the **Precipitation Clerk** (the Tally officer sprite) guards the Clamp with
  Form 12-R ("Application to Request Weather"). Nobody has finished one. Mid-quest, the group
  chat pings: Connor approves the clamp from somewhere ("ya keep it dry, festival vibes. might
  be a bit late to check on it"). First time we see him *running* something.
- **Bring the storm back** (the quest, each step a field move so you switch):
  1. **Look** at the lantern (any friend): Wardeye is *Sighted*. Its entry opens as a **quest**
     page: "Its light is out. Something it watched for stopped coming."
  2. **Callum, Scan** on the rod's plate: KEEPERS' RELAY 2/4 (follows the CRT stone's 1/4),
     and the rod's job: "CALLS THE STORM. DO NOT CLAMP."
  3. **Haydon, Taunt** the clerk off the Clamp ("bro's form has a form").
  4. **Nolan, Patch Up**: unsplices the cable and reconnects the rod with his patch cable.
  5. Cutscene: the Clamp stamps one last cloud and jams; thunder; lightning hits the rod; the
     first rain in years. The lantern **relights**, and a **Storm Shard** drops at the ruins.
     PATCH: "Weather: restored. I did not have weather on the list. I'm adding it to the list."
- After that, Wardeye waits at the rod in the rain: talk to it to battle (level 9) and seal it.
  If it faints it relights the next time it rains.

## Engine work
1. **Evolution triggers:** `evolves` becomes a list of triggers, each with `level` or `item`, plus
   optional conditions (`weather`, `bond`, `flag`). Branching lines = more than one trigger.
   Bag gets a USE action for evolution items, which plays the existing evolve scene.
2. **Weather:** per-map weather (Route 1: none until `storm_returned`, then rain 40%), encounter
   tables per weather, rain overlay on map and battle, the battle effects above.
3. **Static encounters:** a Patchling you talk to (Wardeye), with its own level and conditions.
4. **Quest pages in the bestiary:** quest steps shown in Wardeye's entry, ticked as you go.
5. **Shinies and Prototypes:** rolled per wild encounter, saved on the Patchling (cosmetic only).
   Shiny: a second palette per line (I'll make 3 options per line, you pick on the review sheet),
   sparkle when it appears, star in the bestiary, "shiny sealed". Prototype: washed-out palette +
   live glitch (colour-split edges, scanlines, torn slices), corrupted sound, PATCH's line.
6. Abilities Thunderhead and Unblinking; Severance's "was hit" boost; Vigil Beam's reveal.
7. Attack animations (`moveFx.ts`): **Horn Bolt** (club swing, bolt arc), **Severance** (axe
   overhead, split-screen slash, arcs), **Vigil Beam** (lighthouse sweep across the foe).
