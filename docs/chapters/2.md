# Chapter 2: the Saltway and Kettleport, "Have a Seat" (pitch, 2026-10-30)

Route 2 from Route 1's Tally checkpoint down the coast to Kettleport. **One reply approves it**
(or names what to change). Owner's calls already in: Deputy Reliable is Parker Colt; Connor is the
game's climax and has **8 Generals** (people the group knows; Parker is #1, Hansen #2); Chris Hansen is a fight; Sergey's
trenches; the six Lantern Loft paintings; every town has its own rules.

## What makes Kettleport different (towns don't share one structure)
Hollow Rest was a village with a quest chain of field moves. Kettleport is **a town you rebuild**:
- **It's stacked.** Streets are container roofs at three heights, joined by ladders and gangways.
- **The crane re-stacks the town.** The dock crane picks up containers and puts them down
  somewhere else. Paths open and close, so the town is a **crane puzzle** (a side-view grid: lift
  one container at a time, gravity drops it). The story puzzle opens the harbour; small optional
  ones open alleys with items.
- **Rent goes by height.** The Tally charges "stack rent" per floor, so the rich live on top and
  everyone else lives at the bottom. NPC jokes run on it.
- **Ferries are fast travel.** The board lists every port as RAIN CHECK until its chapter opens.
  In Chapter 2 it sails to a new **jetty below Hollow Rest** (a small map edit there).
- **The tide moves** once it's back: high tide by day, low tide at night (day/night cycle below).
  Low tide opens tide pools with their own encounters.

One line each on the later towns so they don't copy this one (no detail yet): Emberwick, glass you
blow and shape to solve things; Canopy, falling as a way of getting around; Nightmarket, a town that
moves every night; Hush, no talking (you get through on gestures).

## Story beats
1. **The checkpoint (end of Route 1).** The barrier now has someone behind it: **Parker Colt** in a
   Tally uniform, two sizes too neat. He's **Deputy Reliable**. Lore (owner's call): Parker Colt is
   the account itself. When Noah deleted it he got dumped in Verrow. He's the only Tally employee
   who shows up on time because he still keeps a posting schedule ("golden hour waits for no one").
   He leaves his Hollow Rest spot; a FOLLOW @PARKERCOLT poster stays behind.
   - He's waiting for "the Director's road inspection." The group chat pings, Connor: "might be a
     bit late guys" ... "parker's got birds. rock beats birds i think" (the trainer-battle hint).
   - An hour later Connor sends "rain check. something came up". Parker hands over a ticket:
     **DIRECTOR'S INSPECTION: RESCHEDULED. DATE: TBD.** "He's never come. Not once. I still
     post the photos."
   - Parker does the inspection himself: **the first trainer battle and General #1** (a small
     one; he's the tutorial). When he loses he lifts the
     barrier ("tag me in the post").
2. **Route 2, the Saltway.** A cliff road down to beaches, with tall grass, wading shallows and rocks.
   - **A Tally toll booth** (where money comes in): pay 100 Bits, or switch to Haydon to argue; the
     officer cracks and demands a battle, and if you win you walk through free.
   - Five trainers (below). Two field-move spots, not a chain: Dani shoves a fallen container off
     the road; Nolan fixes a tide gate to reach a beach with an item.
3. **Kettleport.** The harbour water is flat and grey and doesn't move. The Tally has stacked a
   wall of impounded containers across the harbour mouth, **the Backlog** (each one "pending
   review"). The tide can't come in, the ferries can't sail, and the harbour Patchlings are going
   quiet. Only the crane can move the Backlog, and its operator never leaves the cab.
4. **Get Sergey out of the trenches.** The crane operator is **Sergey**, 19, Russian, Callum's
   producer, on his 400th night shift and making beats on the crane radio. He can't clock out
   without a stamp from the Tally harbour office, and the office can't stamp anything until the form
   for the stamp has been stamped.
   - The four run the loop (Haydon distracts the clerk, Callum Scans the form's fine print and finds
     it's stamped itself, Dani carries Sergey's 40 kg of speakers). Sergey walks out into daylight
     for the first time: "Is bright. I go back now?"
   - He hands over the crane keys and plays Callum's old track on the crane radio: *"and I picked up
     a mic and I'm chill."* The guys never let it go again.
   - (Sergey will be back in some other trenches every chapter. Running gag.)
5. **The crane puzzle.** Clear the Backlog to open the harbour mouth. The last container opens
   by itself...
6. **The sting.** **Chris Hansen** steps out of it, the Tally's **General of Licensing**, General #2. A chair in a lit container, two cameras. *"Why don't you have a seat right
   over there."* He's running "To Catch a Sealer" and has a file on each of them: Callum's 3 sloppy
   seals, Nolan's unlicensed patch cable, Dani's 4 Patchlings "befriended without paperwork". Haydon's
   file is all **[REDACTED]**. Haydon: "Man like Hansen."
   - Group chat before the fight: Connor, "hansen's got a dark one so like. bring orbitot? idk i'm
     not there". **Boss fight** (team of three). Win, and he packs the chair: "We'll be in touch."
7. **The tide comes back.** The water rushes in, the harbour moves again, and the ferries start.
   Something long and striped surfaces by the old tide stair: **Tidemark** (the rare/quest one
   below). Keepers' **Relay 3/4** is carved on the stair. The **Tide Gallery** opens.
   PATCH: "Tides: restored. I did put the moon in. I just never told it to do anything."

Connor stays a voice in the chat this chapter. The real fights with him are the game's climax.

## The four new lines (dex 22-29)
Every stage below gets concept art, front + back sprites, its own idle and a walking sheet
(WORKFLOW: whole lines).

### Tinnip → Crateclaw → Gantrix (Water → Water/Metal → Water/Metal): the container crab
- **Tinnip**: a little hermit crab living in a dented food tin, its lid flipped up like a visor.
  Rust-orange claws, bubble eyes on stalks, one claw way bigger than the other. It slams the lid
  shut when it's scared. Pose: 3/4 left, big claw raised, lid half open.
- **Crateclaw**: it's outgrown the tin and moved into a **shipping container** shell, ribbed steel
  with its own little doors that it bangs open to attack. Tally rent tags stuck all over it.
- **Gantrix**: a huge dock crab whose big claw has become a **crane hook on a cable** and whose
  shell is a gantry with a little control-cab window. Hazard stripes and rivets.
- Story hook: Kettleport was built copying their shells. The Tally charges them "shell rent"
  (bestiary: "Has been evicted from its own body twice").
- Stats: Tinnip 45/60/75/30/45/35 (290), Crateclaw 65/80/100/40/60/45 (390),
  Gantrix 85/110/120/55/75/50 (495). Evolves at 16 and 34.
- Ability **Hunker Down**: below half HP, Defense x1.5 (it shuts the lid).
- Moves: Nip (Water 40), **Lid Slam** at 5 (Metal physical 55/95, may flinch), Bubble Wall
  (raises Defense), Door Charge at Crateclaw (Metal 75). Signature **Hook & Haul** at Gantrix (Metal
  physical 90/95: hooks the foe and drops it; the foe can't switch or run next turn).

### Grubgull → Heistern (Water/Wind → Wind/Dark): the gull who robs you
- **Grubgull**: a fat, scruffy seagull chick, always mid-chew, with crumbs on its beak and a chip
  in its foot. White and grey, yellow beak, beady eyes. Pose: waddling forward, wings out, mouth full.
- **Heistern**: a sleek tern in a **burglar look**: black eye-mask feathers, striped chest like a
  robber's jumper, a sack-shaped throat pouch full of loot. Pose: mid-swoop with a stolen burger.
- Story hook: it steals the Triple Cheese out of Callum's hands in a cutscene on the Saltway, and
  the Galley diner has its mugshot on the wall. Bestiary: "Has never paid for a single chip."
- Stats: Grubgull 55/55/45/45/45/75 (320), Heistern 75/95/65/60/65/115 (475). Evolves at 20.
- Ability **Scavenger**: after a battle it sometimes brings back an item or some Bits.
- Moves: Peck (Wind 35), Gulp (heals a bit), Gust (Wind 50), Squawk (lowers Attack). Signature
  **Smash & Grab** at Heistern (Dark physical 75/100, goes first, steals the foe's stat raises).

### Helmink → Dredgenaut (Metal/Water): the diving-helmet octopus (uncommon, night only)
- **Helmink**: a small octopus living in an antique **brass diving helmet**. Its eyes glow through
  the round porthole, tentacles curl out of the neck ring, and it leaves ink smudges. It only comes
  up onto the piers at night. Shy.
- **Dredgenaut**: a whole brass **diving suit** filled with octopus. Tentacles come out at the
  joints, it has lead boots and drags an anchor on a chain. Slow, heavy, unstoppable.
- Story hook: it salvaged sunken wrecks for the old keepers' Tide Gallery (the gallery's shells and
  glass are its finds). Since the harbour went still it's been hoarding them under the pier.
- Stats: Helmink 50/40/75/65/65/25 (320), Dredgenaut 90/70/115/95/100/20 (490).
  Evolves at 24 **at night** (a new time condition on the evolution triggers).
- Ability **Ink Cloud**: a contact hit lowers the attacker's accuracy.
- Moves: Bubble (Water 40), Porthole Glare (lowers Speed), Ink Spray (Water 55, lowers accuracy),
  Anchor Drop (Metal 80/90). Signature **Pressure Crush** (Water special: up to 120 power, stronger
  the more HP the foe has left).

### Tidemark (Water/Relic, single stage): the harbour's tide-counter (rare/quest)
- A long, sleek **harbour seal** (the animal, which is the joke: you seal a seal). Down its side
  are white **tide-gauge marks in groups of four with a slash**, the same mark as the Tally crest.
  Calm, old eyes, whiskers beaded with water.
- Story hook: it counted the tides for the keepers, and the Tally copied its marks for their crest
  when PATCH's first draft built them. It's the first time the Tally's crest is traced back
  (ecology/history thread). It went dormant when the harbour went still.
- **Quest**: bring the tide back (the Backlog). Then it waits at the tide stair at high tide:
  battle at lv 16 and seal it.
- Stats 80/60/80/95/90/65 (470). Ability **Tide Count**: every 4th turn its move hits twice as hard.
- Moves: Water Pulse, Flipper Slap (Water physical 60), Undertow (lowers Speed). Signature **Spring
  Tide** (Water special: 60 power, +20 each use in the battle, up to 140).

6. **Day/night** (above).
7. **Crane puzzle** scene: data-driven puzzles (story + optional alleys).
8. **Ferries**: fast travel from the board, ports unlock by flag; Hollow Rest jetty.
9. **Maps**: Route 2, Kettleport on three heights (ladders/gangways), interiors.
10. **The new lines**: abilities, moves, quest page for Tidemark, attack animations for Hook &
