## What makes Kettleport different (towns don't share one structure)
Hollow Rest was a village with a quest chain of field moves. Kettleport is **a town you rebuild**:
- **It's stacked.** Streets are container roofs at three heights, joined by ladders and gangways.
- **The crane re-stacks the town.** The dock crane picks up containers and puts them down
  somewhere else. Paths open and close, so the town is a **crane puzzle** (a side-view grid: lift
  one container at a time, gravity drops it). The story puzzle opens the harbour; small optional
  ones open alleys with items.
- **Rent goes by height.** The Tally charges "stack rent" per floor, so the rich live on top and
  everyone else lives at the bottom. NPC jokes run on it.
- **Ferries are fast travel.** The board lists every port as RAIN CHECK until its chapter opens.
  In Chapter 2 it sails to a new **jetty below Hollow Rest** (a small map edit there).
- **The tide moves** once it's back: high tide by day, low tide at night (day/night cycle below).
  Low tide opens tide pools with their own encounters.

One line each on the later towns so they don't copy this one (no detail yet): Emberwick, glass you
blow and shape to solve things; Canopy, falling as a way of getting around; Nightmarket, a town that
moves every night; Hush, no talking (you get through on gestures).

### Tinnip → Crateclaw → Gantrix (Water → Water/Metal → Water/Metal): the container crab

- **Gantrix**: a huge dock crab whose big claw has become a **crane hook on a cable** and whose
  shell is a gantry with a little control-cab window. Hazard stripes and rivets.
- Story hook: Kettleport was built copying their shells. The Tally charges them "shell rent"
  (bestiary: "Has been evicted from its own body twice").
- Stats: Tinnip 45/60/75/30/45/35 (290), Crateclaw 65/80/100/40/60/45 (390),

- Moves: Nip (Water 40), **Lid Slam** at 5 (Metal physical 55/95, may flinch), Bubble Wall

  physical 90/95: hooks the foe and drops it; the foe can't switch or run next turn).

### Grubgull → Heistern (Water/Wind → Wind/Dark): the gull who robs you
- **Grubgull**: a fat, scruffy seagull chick, always mid-chew, with crumbs on its beak and a chip
  in its foot. White and grey, yellow beak, beady eyes. Pose: waddling forward, wings out, mouth full.
- **Heistern**: a sleek tern in a **burglar look**: black eye-mask feathers, striped chest like a
  robber's jumper, a sack-shaped throat pouch full of loot. Pose: mid-swoop with a stolen burger.
- Story hook: it steals the Triple Cheese out of Callum's hands in a cutscene on the Saltway, and
  the Galley diner has its mugshot on the wall. Bestiary: "Has never paid for a single chip."
- Stats: Grubgull 55/55/45/45/45/75 (320), Heistern 75/95/65/60/65/115 (475). Evolves at 20.
- Ability **Scavenger**: after a battle it sometimes brings back an item or some Bits.
- Moves: Peck (Wind 35), Gulp (heals a bit), Gust (Wind 50), Squawk (lowers Attack). Signature
  **Smash & Grab** at Heistern (Dark physical 75/100, goes first, steals the foe's stat raises).

### Helmink → Dredgenaut (Metal/Water): the diving-helmet octopus (uncommon, night only)
- **Helmink**: a small octopus living in an antique **brass diving helmet**. Its eyes glow through
  the round porthole, tentacles curl out of the neck ring, and it leaves ink smudges. It only comes
  up onto the piers at night. Shy.
- **Dredgenaut**: a whole brass **diving suit** filled with octopus. Tentacles come out at the

- Story hook: it salvaged sunken wrecks for the old keepers' Tide Gallery (the gallery's shells and
  glass are its finds). Since the harbour went still it's been hoarding them under the pier.
- Stats: Helmink 50/40/75/65/65/25 (320), Dredgenaut 90/70/115/95/100/20 (490).

- Ability **Ink Cloud**: a contact hit lowers the attacker's accuracy.
- Moves: Bubble (Water 40), Porthole Glare (lowers Speed), Ink Spray (Water 55, lowers accuracy),
  Anchor Drop (Metal 80/90). Signature **Pressure Crush** (Water special: up to 120 power, stronger
  the more HP the foe has left).

### Tidemark (Water/Relic, single stage): the harbour's tide-counter (rare/quest)
- A long, sleek **harbour seal** (the animal, which is the joke: you seal a seal). Down its side
  are white **tide-gauge marks in groups of four with a slash**, the same mark as the Tally crest.
  Calm, old eyes, whiskers beaded with water.
- Story hook: it counted the tides for the keepers, and the Tally copied its marks for their crest

- Stats 80/60/80/95/90/65 (470). Ability **Tide Count**: every 4th turn its move hits twice as hard.
- Moves: Water Pulse, Flipper Slap (Water physical 60), Undertow (lowers Speed). Signature **Spring
  Tide** (Water special: 60 power, +20 each use in the battle, up to 140).
