## Kame House Patchlings: pitch (approve before prompts 16-19)
Two single-stage Patchlings for the Easter-egg islet (beat Master Roshi, then they turn up on the islet). Original designs, inspired by the franchise. Say "change X" or "go" before you run 16-19.
- **Kettleback** (Water/Rock): a stocky old sea turtle whose shell is a huge iron **kettlebell** (the handle arches over its back), round little dark sunglasses, a wispy white chin beard. Trains all day by carrying it. Bestiary: "Has been 'about to take the weight off' for 300 years." Pose: 3/4 left, mid-squat, straining under the shell. Ability **Iron Shell** (survives one hit from full HP). Signature **Weight Off**: drops the kettlebell, Defense falls 2, Speed rises 2 and Attack 1.
- **Nimbuddy** (Wind): a puffy golden cloud with a sleepy, happy face and two little cloud arms, a wisp tail. It only carries the pure of heart. Bestiary: "Dani rode it first try. Haydon has fallen through it four times." Pose: 3/4 left, swooping low like it's offering a ride. Ability **Pure Heart** (Dark moves miss it). Signature **Cloud Hop** (Wind physical, hops out of reach: it moves first next turn). Later: a ride over water.
